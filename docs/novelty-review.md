# Targeted novelty review

Checked primary sources on 3 October 2026. This is a scoped comparison, not proof that no prior paper has proposed a similar right. Publication status should be checked again before submission.

| Prior work | Established overlap | What still needs to distinguish this proposal |
|---|---|---|
| [Conformity in Large Language Models, ACL 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.195/) | Majority influence and interventions | Do not claim discovery of conformity; isolate a controller-enforced obligation |
| [Voting or Consensus?, Findings ACL 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.606/) | Comparison of decision protocols | More than another prompt or aggregation-rule comparison |
| [Debate or Vote?, NeurIPS 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.17536) | Strong voting baseline and analysis of debate | Demonstrate opportunities and changed decisions beyond ensembling |
| [Free-MAD, 2025 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.11035) | Anti-conformity and trajectory-based aggregation | Preserving standing after endorsement changes, rather than only scoring trajectories |
| [Hear Both Sides, 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20640) | Propagation of differing original arguments | Distinguish retention of evidence from mandatory recorded disposition |
| [Multi-agent discussion gains less when dissent is withheld, 29 September 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38324) | Withholding, activation, and instructions encouraging dissent | No claim that merely eliciting dissent or modelling its absence is new |
| [Singh, A Social Semantics for Agent Communication Languages, 1999](https://repository.lib.ncsu.edu/items/66b06379-fab6-4894-994c-66b2465414ca) | Public commitments as semantics for interaction | Position as an application and research agenda grounded in existing institutions |

Candidate contribution: specify and study a challenge lifecycle whose obligations survive the author's conversational change of answer, link that lifecycle to action authorization, and evaluate opportunity, activation, disposition and harmful review separately. This is a proposed distinction, not an established first-of-its-kind claim. The full related-work review should additionally cover argumentation dialogue/commitment stores, normative agent middleware, and contestable AI systems; the present draft must not imply these fields were comprehensively excluded.

The crucial falsifier is a matched controller with identical evidence and identical review allocation: if the only difference is the name of the right, there is no distinct mechanism. In an all-disagreement diagnostic dataset, persistent and unconditional review coincide; this is acknowledged, not hidden. Persistent review can worsen correct decisions and cause budget-related abstention. Such outcomes must remain reportable.
